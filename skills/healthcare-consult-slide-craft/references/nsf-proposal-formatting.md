# NSF Proposal Formatting Rules

Use this reference when creating decks, slides, or recommendations related to NSF proposals, Principal Investigator support tools, agentic AI for proposal writing, or research administration involving NSF funding.

**Always note:** The specific program solicitation supersedes the general PAPPG rules. Current base document is PAPPG 24-1 (effective for proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024), with later supplements.

## Core Formatting Requirements (PAPPG 24-1, Chapter II.C.2)

These rules apply to all uploaded sections of a proposal, including supplementary documentation.

| Requirement              | Rule                                                                 | Notes |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|-------|
| Paper size               | Standard letter (8.5" × 11") or landscape (11" × 8.5")               | Landscape now explicitly allowed |
| Margins                  | At least 1 inch on all sides                                         | No proposer-supplied information may appear in the margins |
| Font options             | • Arial, Courier New, or Palatino Linotype ≥ 10 pt<br>• Times New Roman or Computer Modern ≥ 11 pt | Arial Narrow is **not** permitted |
| Line spacing / density   | No more than 6 lines of text per vertical inch                       | Critical compliance check |
| Smaller fonts            | Allowed for mathematical formulas, equations, figures, tables, diagram captions, and Symbol font for Greek/special characters | Must remain readable |
| Column format            | Single-column strongly encouraged                                    | Multi-column can hinder electronic review |
| Pagination               | Do not add page numbers (Research.gov adds them)                     | — |

**Compliance risk:** Proposals that violate font, spacing, or margin rules may be returned without review.

## Key Page Limits (Standard Research Proposals)

| Section                              | Typical Limit                          | Notes |
|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|-------|
| Project Summary                      | 1 page                                 | Must contain three separately labeled sections: Overview, Intellectual Merit, Broader Impacts. Each heading on its own line. |
| Project Description                  | 15 pages                               | Includes all figures, tables, and charts. Results from Prior NSF Support counts against this limit (max 5 pages within the 15). |
| Budget Justification                 | 5 pages                                | Separate 5-page limit for each subaward organization |
| Mentoring Plan                       | 1 page                                 | Required for postdoctoral researchers **and** graduate students supported on the project |
| Data Management and Sharing Plan     | Structured webform (Research.gov)      | As of April 27, 2026, no longer a free-text 2-page PDF upload; uses a structured webform |
| Biographical Sketch                  | SciENcv format                         | Synergistic Activities section removed from biosketch (now separate) |

**Important:** Always check the specific solicitation. Some mechanisms (RAPID, EAGER, CAREER, etc.) have different limits or additional required documents (e.g., Departmental Letter for CAREER).

## Project Summary Structure (Strict)

Must fit on **one page** and contain exactly these three sections, each starting with the heading alone on its own line:

1. **Overview**
2. **Intellectual Merit**
3. **Broader Impacts**

Written in the third person. Missing or unlabeled sections can cause the proposal to be returned without review.

## Project Description Requirements

- Must include a separate section labeled **Broader Impacts**.
- If the PI/co-PI has received NSF support with an end date in the past five years (or current funding), a **Results from Prior NSF Support** section is required (limited to 5 pages within the overall 15-page limit).
- No URLs permitted in the Project Description in most cases.

## Budget Justification Rules (Detailed)

**Page limit**: Maximum **5 pages** per organization.  
Each subaward organization must also submit its own separate Budget Justification of no more than 5 pages.

**Core requirement**: Every line item on the budget must be documented and justified.

### Key Content Rules

| Category | What must be justified / noted |
|----------|--------------------------------|
| **Senior/Key Personnel** | Names, person-months, and salary amounts. NSF generally limits compensation to **no more than 2 months** of regular salary per year from all NSF sources combined. Any request above 2 months must be explicitly disclosed and justified. The organization must define what constitutes a “year” and state that definition in the justification. |
| **Other Personnel** | Postdocs, other professionals, graduate/undergraduate students — rates of pay and person-months or total amounts. |
| **Equipment** | Each item listed individually by description and estimated cost. Must be research equipment not already available; general-purpose items usually not allowed as direct costs. |
| **Travel** | Itemized by destination and cost. Relation to the project must be clear. International travel should list countries (and dates if known). |
| **Participant Support** | Number of participants; note that speakers/trainers are generally not participants. Certain costs (room rental, catering for conferences, research subject incentives) have restrictions. |
| **Other Direct Costs** | Materials & supplies, publication costs, consultant services (include expertise, affiliation, daily rate, number of days), computer services, subawards, etc. |
| **Indirect Costs (F&A)** | The negotiated rate(s), the base(s) to which they are applied, and the total amount of indirect costs **must** be specified in the Budget Justification. |
| **Cost Sharing** | Voluntary committed cost sharing is generally **not** allowed unless required by the solicitation. Do not include it. |

### Additional Important Points

- The Budget Justification is used by NSF to determine the final award amount.
- All costs must be necessary, reasonable, allocable, and allowable under 2 CFR 200 and NSF policy.
- Confidential salary information for senior personnel can be requested (appear as a single figure on the public budget; detailed statement submitted separately).
- Collaborative proposals: each organization submits its own 5-page Budget Justification.

## Practical Implications for Agentic AI / PI Support Tools

When recommending or designing agentic AI solutions that support NSF proposals:

- Enforce or guide users on the stricter 1-inch margins and the dual font-size rules (10 pt vs 11 pt depending on typeface).
- Automatically structure the Project Summary with the three required labeled headings.
- Track the 15-page Project Description budget in real time (including figures).
- Flag Results from Prior NSF Support length.
- Support the newer structured Data Management and Sharing Plan webform content.
- Generate compliant single-column, correctly spaced output that passes Research.gov checks.
- For Budget Justification: enforce the 5-page limit, require definition of a “year”, flag any senior personnel request > 2 months, ensure every budget line is justified, and include the F&A rate/base/total.
