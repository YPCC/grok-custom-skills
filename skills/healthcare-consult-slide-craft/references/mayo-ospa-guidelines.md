# Mayo Clinic OSPA (Office of Sponsored Projects Administration) Guidelines

Reference for agentic AI / consulting systems that support Principal Investigators at Mayo Clinic (or similar academic medical centers). OSPA is the central institutional office that sits between the PI/department and external sponsors (NIH, foundations, industry, etc.).

**Important caveat**: Full internal SOPs, exact internal deadlines, and system access rules are not fully public. This document synthesizes publicly available information (SRA presentations describing Mayo processes, Mayo COI policy, job descriptions, and standard academic medical center practice consistent with the provided materials). Always verify current internal deadlines and requirements with the assigned OSPA Sponsored Project Specialist (SPS).

## 1. Role of OSPA vs Department

| Party | Primary Responsibilities |
|-------|--------------------------|
| **Department / PI** | Scientific content, scientific review, identification of cost-sharing or F&A waiver needs, alignment with institutional priorities, preparation of non-budgetary documents (aims, research strategy, biosketches, Other Support, etc.) |
| **OSPA (Central Office)** | Reviews RFAs / NOFOs, assists with budget development (often in MIRIS), validates institutional compliance, performs final package review, and is the **official submitting body** to external sponsors (whether Mayo is prime or subrecipient) |

OSPA staff titles commonly include Sponsored Project Specialist (SPS). The SPS is the primary point of contact for a given proposal.

## 2. Typical Proposal Workflow (Publicly Described)

1. PI / Department identifies a funding opportunity and notifies the assigned OSPA SPS.
2. SPS provides a Funding Proposal Template / MIRIS questions and communicates the **internal deadline**.
3. Simultaneously, Research Support Staff (RSS) or equivalent work with the PI on non-budgetary components and create a shared working folder.
4. Budget is developed with OSPA assistance (frequently inside MIRIS).
5. Non-budgetary documents are finalized and uploaded into MIRIS.
6. SPS composes the full proposal package, performs compliance review, and sends to PI for final review/approval.
7. OSPA submits the official package to the external sponsor.

**Key rule**: OSPA is the only entity authorized to submit official proposals on behalf of Mayo Clinic in most cases.

## 3. Internal Deadlines

- OSPA sets internal routing and processing deadlines that are **earlier** than the external sponsor deadline.
- These internal deadlines apply to federal, foundation, and subrecipient submissions.
- Exact number of business days is not publicly fixed in the materials reviewed, but standard practice at similar institutions is 5–10 business days; Mayo materials emphasize that internal deadlines “must be strictly met.”
- Late internal submission risks missing the external deadline.

**Agentic AI implication**: Always prompt the user for the OSPA-assigned internal deadline and treat it as the hard stop, not the sponsor deadline.

## 4. Budget Preparation

- Budgets are typically developed or finalized with OSPA assistance inside **MIRIS** (Mayo’s research information system).
- OSPA reviews for institutional compliance, correct F&A rates, cost-sharing, salary cap application, and allowability.
- Department is responsible for identifying any cost-sharing or F&A waiver requests early.

## 5. Key Compliance Items OSPA Reviews

### Other Support / Current & Pending Support
- Must accurately detail all active and pending support, consulting, appointments, and in-kind resources.
- Requires OSPA review before submission.
- Alignment with NIH Common Forms (SciENcv) expectations is required for NIH applications.

### Biosketches
- Must be accurate and complete; OSPA reviews as part of the package.

### Conflict of Interest (COI)
- All research personnel (PI, Co-Is, key personnel, and others responsible for design/conduct/reporting) must adhere to Mayo’s PHS/NIH-aligned COI policy.
- Financial eDisclosure required.
- Disclosures cover the investigator, spouse/domestic partner, and dependent children.
- Significant Financial Interests (including foreign) must be disclosed; management plans approved by the Conflict of Interest Review Board before award expenditure or related protocol activation.
- New interests must be reported within 30 days; NIH notification timelines apply for PHS-funded work.

### Other Institutional Checks
- Regulatory / policy / procedural items.
- Correct institutional signatures and authorized organizational representative (AOR) actions.
- Consistency with Mayo policies on human subjects, animal use, etc., when applicable.

## 6. Systems Mentioned

- **MIRIS** — Primary system for funding proposal development, budget, and internal routing at Mayo.
- Shared drive / working folders for collaborative document preparation.
- External portals (ASSIST, Grants.gov, Research.gov, foundation portals) are used for final submission, but OSPA performs the official submission in most cases.

## 7. Implications for Agentic AI / PI Support Tools

When supporting a Mayo Clinic PI (or similar OSPA-mediated institution):

- Treat OSPA as a mandatory intermediate gate, not an optional advisor.
- Surface the need to notify the assigned SPS early.
- Require the internal OSPA deadline as the controlling deadline.
- Generate budgets that are ready for MIRIS / OSPA review (correct categories, salary-cap aware, F&A correct).
- Produce Other Support and Biosketch drafts that are complete and ready for OSPA compliance review.
- Flag COI disclosure status and remind users of the 30-day update rule.
- Never assume the PI can submit directly to NIH/ASSIST without OSPA involvement unless explicitly confirmed.
- Provide a checklist of “documents OSPA will expect” (final science, budget, justifications, Other Support, biosketches, COI clearances, etc.).
