# Critique Checklist

Use this structured evaluation for Critique mode and as an internal gate for Update and Draft modes.

## 1. Narrative Structure

- Does the deck follow a clear SCR (Situation–Complication–Resolution) arc?
- Is there a strong executive summary that states the recommendation upfront?
- Would a busy healthcare executive understand the core argument from the first 2–3 slides alone?
- Is the ordering logical? Does each slide build on the previous one?
- Are sections MECE?

## 2. Action Titles

- Read only the titles in sequence. Do they tell a coherent story?
- Is every title a complete sentence with a clear takeaway?
- Are any titles still topic titles (“Market Overview”, “Q3 Results”, “Competitive Landscape”)? Rewrite them.
- Do titles stay within ~15 words / two lines?

## 3. Slide-Level Logic

- Does each slide make exactly one point (the point in its title)?
- Does every element on the slide support that title?
- Are there slides that try to do too much? Recommend splitting.
- Are there redundant slides that could be merged or cut?
- Does every slide pass the “so what?” test?

## 4. Evidence Quality

- Are charts the right type for the insight?
- Do charts have clear labels, units, and visual hierarchy that draws the eye to the key insight?
- Is data cited with credible, reasonably recent sources?
- Are assumptions stated when material?
- Are frameworks well-adapted rather than generic?

## 5. Visual Discipline (Hybrid Design System)

- Pure white background and correct hybrid palette?
- Consistent fonts, title sizing, and alignment across the deck?
- Generous whitespace and clean margins?
- Minimal clutter? Decorative elements removed?
- Sources present and consistently placed?
- For infographics: symmetry, flat icons, thin connectors, high readability?

## 6. Healthcare Context Fit

- Language precise and appropriate for mixed clinical + executive audiences?
- Domain frameworks used correctly (journey maps, TAM/SAM/SOM, care pathways, RAG, etc.)?
- Avoids both over-simplification and unnecessary jargon?

## Critique Output Format

Present findings in three tiers:

1. **Structural issues** — Narrative arc, missing sections, ordering, executive summary
2. **Slide-level issues** — Specific slides, with concrete before/after title rewrites and recommended splits/merges
3. **Polish items** — Formatting, chart choice, sourcing, consistency, visual refinements

For every issue: current state → problem → specific fix.
