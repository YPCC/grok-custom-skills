# Firm Variants

Default is always the clean hybrid defined in `design-system.md`.

Switch only when the user explicitly requests pure McKinsey, BCG, or Bain style.

## Quick Comparison

| Element              | Hybrid (Default)          | Pure McKinsey                  | Pure BCG                       | Pure Bain                      |
|----------------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Visual density       | Balanced                  | Text-heavier, structured bullets | Chart-heavy, sparse text      | Balanced, practical            |
| Action titles        | Complete sentence, punchy | Often more comprehensive / with logical subtext | Punchy, quantitative          | Clean, direct                  |
| Color accent         | Teal (#00A3A1)            | Blue-heavy                     | Green accent                   | Red accent                     |
| Typical feel         | Modern consulting hybrid  | Rigorous written argument      | Visual, “show don’t tell”      | Implementation-oriented        |
| Exhibit style        | Clear callouts + clean charts | Detailed annotations         | Bold callouts on key data      | Selective emphasis             |

## How to Switch

1. Confirm the request (“McKinsey style”, “pure BCG”, “Bain deck”, etc.).
2. Adjust:
   - Color accent (blue / green / red)
   - Text vs visual density
   - Title length and tone
3. Keep the non-negotiable principles (action titles, Pyramid, one message, SCR, sourcing, MECE) identical across all variants.
4. Note the chosen variant clearly when delivering.

## Guidance for Healthcare Contexts

- Hybrid works extremely well for most healthcare consulting work (provider strategy, digital health, value-based care, market access, operational improvement).
- Pure McKinsey style is often preferred for complex, multi-stakeholder strategy arguments that need written logic.
- Pure BCG style shines when the story is strongly data-driven and visual (e.g., market maps, pathway analytics, performance dashboards).
- Pure Bain style is useful when the emphasis is on practical implementation, results, and clear next steps.
